FAQ

Common Questions

How quickly will the office reply?

Messages are answered within one working day. Urgent matters should be called in to the security desk.

Where can I pay my maintenance?

Login to the resident dashboard and pay online by UPI, card or net banking. You can also pay at the office during working hours.

How do I raise a complaint?

Use the Complaints section of your dashboard. You will get a reference number and can track the status or escalate.

Can visitors park at the society?

Visitor parking is available at the main gate. Guests staying longer than four hours should register at the security desk.

Who do I contact in an emergency?

Call the 24×7 security desk on +91 80 4567 8901 or the emergency line on +91 80 4567 8999.

Prefer to Do It Online?

Pay dues, book the clubhouse and raise complaints from your resident dashboard.
