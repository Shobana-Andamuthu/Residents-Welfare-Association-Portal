About Our Association

Twelve years of resident-led community management at Maple Heights.

Our Story

A Community Built on Trust

Maple Heights RWA was formed in 2014 when the first residents moved in. Today it brings together 480 families across three towers to manage security, maintenance and shared spaces.

Every decision is taken by an elected committee and shared openly with residents through notices, meetings and this portal.

What We Stand For

Our Mission & Values

Three principles guide every decision of the committee, from the annual budget to a broken streetlight.

“A good community is built one considerate neighbour at a time.”

Safety First

Secure entry, trained guards and regular drills keep every family protected.

Transparency

Accounts, budgets and minutes are shared with all residents.

Community Spirit

We encourage neighbours to meet, celebrate and help one another.

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Way

  • 2014

    Association registered and first 120 families moved in.

  • 2016

    Clubhouse and swimming pool opened to residents.

How We Govern

Open and Accountable

Clear processes keep the community running smoothly.

Annual Election

Committee members are elected by residents every year.

Monthly Meetings

The committee meets monthly and publishes the minutes.

Audited Accounts

An external auditor reviews accounts every financial year.

Resident Voice

Suggestions and complaints are reviewed in every meeting.
