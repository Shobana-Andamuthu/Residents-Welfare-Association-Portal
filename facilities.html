Usage Guidelines

Please Help Us Keep Facilities Great

Bookings must be made 3 days ahead and cancelled 24 hours before the slot.

Respect Neighbours

FAQ

Facility Questions

Who can book the clubhouse?

Any registered resident with no pending maintenance dues can book the clubhouse for private events.

How far ahead can I book?

Slots can be booked up to 60 days ahead and at least 3 days before the event.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Cancel from your dashboard at least 24 hours before the slot for a full refund of the slot fee.

Are outside caterers allowed?

Yes, with prior intimation to the facility manager. Cooking with open flame is not permitted inside the hall.

Are the pool and gym free?

Yes. They are free for residents. Guests are charged a small fee and must be accompanied by a resident.
